# Faulty Link Labs Email Hosting Plan

**Domain:** `faultylinklabs.com`  
**Date:** 2026-07-26  
**Prepared for:** Petee

## Assumptions

- You own or can register `faultylinklabs.com`.
- Primary goals are privacy, open-source preference, and manageable overhead.
- Expected scale: small team/labs contacts (2–10 mailboxes).
- No strict compliance requirements beyond general data-privacy expectations.

## Option A: Managed Privacy Provider

### Proton Mail for Business

- **Cost:** ~$10–12 USD/user/month (verify current pricing before buying).
- **Setup:** Sign up, add `faultylinklabs.com`, verify ownership, then paste the DNS records Proton provides into your registrar.
- **DNS:** MX, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records are generated by Proton.
- **Privacy:** End-to-end encryption between Proton users; external mail is TLS in transit; Swiss jurisdiction; open-source clients.
- **Ongoing:** Billing and user management only.
- **Best if:** You want email that just works with strong privacy.

### Tutanota Business

- **Cost:** ~$3–6 USD/user/month.
- **Setup:** Same general flow as Proton.
- **Privacy:** German jurisdiction; encrypted subject lines; full open-source clients.
- **Tradeoffs:** Smaller ecosystem, fewer integrations, less polished than Proton.
- **Best if:** Cost matters and you can live with a narrower feature set.

## Option B: Self-Hosted

### Stack: Mailcow

- **Server:** VPS with a static IPv4 and rDNS support.
  - Minimum: 4 GB RAM, 2 vCPU, 40 GB storage.
  - rDNS must point `mail.faultylinklabs.com` to the server IP.
- **Deployment:** `mailcow/mailcow-dockerized` via Docker Compose.
- **DNS records:**
  - `mail.faultylinklabs.com` A/AAAA → server IP
  - `faultylinklabs.com` MX → `mail.faultylinklabs.com` (priority 10)
  - `faultylinklabs.com` TXT → `v=spf1 mx ~all`
  - `default._domainkey.faultylinklabs.com` TXT → DKIM key from Mailcow
  - `_dmarc.faultylinklabs.com` TXT → `v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@faultylinklabs.com; pct=100`
  - PTR/rDNS → `mail.faultylinklabs.com` (set at VPS provider)
- **Setup time:** 1 day if you are comfortable with DNS; 2–3 days if learning.
- **Ongoing:** Weekly OS and container updates, IP-reputation checks, backup verification, and occasional spam-filter tuning.
- **Cost:** ~$6–10/month for the VPS plus domain registration/renewal.

## Comparison

| Criterion | Proton Business | Mailcow Self-Hosted |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Privacy trust | High (Swiss, audited) | Absolute (you hold the data) |
| Workload | Minimal | Moderate ongoing |
| Deliverability | Excellent | Variable; needs reputation management |
| Custom domain | Yes | Yes |
| Open source | Partial (clients/encryption) | Full stack |
| Cost per month | Higher per user | Lower, but time is the real cost |

## Recommendation

For **Faulty Link Labs**, start with **Proton Mail Business** on `faultylinklabs.com`. It gives you a credible privacy posture with near-zero maintenance, which is the right tradeoff while the project is active. Run a **self-hosted Mailcow experiment** only if you later need full data sovereignty or want mail-server operations as a labs project.
